Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026.

English

  • Local processing. Maps are generated in your browser. The generated map itself is never stored on, or sent to, our servers.
  • Third-party map services. To search for places and fetch map data, the coordinates of the area you view and your search terms are sent to OpenStreetMap-based services (OpenStreetMap API / Overpass / Nominatim / tile servers), and to the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (GSI) for Japanese address search. Their handling is subject to their own policies.
  • Local storage. Your selected language and your daily usage count are stored only in your browser (localStorage). We do not read them.
  • Advertising & cookies. The free version may display advertising (e.g. Google AdSense). Ad providers may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads. For EU/EEA/UK visitors, a consent mechanism is provided where required.
  • No accounts. The Service does not require an account and does not collect personal profiles.
  • Contact. Questions: YOUR-EMAIL.
